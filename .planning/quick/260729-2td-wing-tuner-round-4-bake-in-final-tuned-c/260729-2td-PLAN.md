`handlePower(_:)` (`Islet/Notch/NotchWindowController.swift` line 2404) computes
`next = powerActivity(from: reading)`, then (after the one-time launch-seed guard)
`fire = shouldTriggerSplash(previous: lastActivity, next: next)` before setting

to `.onBattery(percent:)`; `shouldTriggerSplash` is `isOnAC(next) && !isOnAC(previous)` — a pure
AC-edge predicate (`isOnAC` is `true` for `.charging`/`.full`, `false` for `.onBattery`/`nil`). Since
this dev machine is very likely already AC-connected (`lastActivity` already `.charging` or
`.full`), the fake charging call above is a same-category tick (`isOnAC(next)=true`,
`isOnAC(previous)=true` → `fire=false`) and silently no-ops — confirmed by
`testSameCategoryTickDoesNotTrigger`/`testTopOffChargingToFullDoesNotTrigger`
(`IsletTests/PowerActivityTests.swift` lines 88-97).
