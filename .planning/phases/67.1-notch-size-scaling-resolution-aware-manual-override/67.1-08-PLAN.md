These four wings have no `interaction.collapsedNotchSize`-derived camera-block term (unlike osdWings/capsLockWings/etc., handled in Plan 09) — their existing fixed half-widths are a hand-tuned safety margin, not a hardware-verified floor. Depth scaling has no such constraint (height has no bearing on horizontal camera clearance) and uses the full `resolvedWingDepthScale` range.

Purpose: Establish the shared D-14 scale contract once, applied first to the simplest (Spacer()-based) wing group, before Plan 09 tackles the six hardware-camera-block wings and Plan 10 tackles the bespoke media wing.
