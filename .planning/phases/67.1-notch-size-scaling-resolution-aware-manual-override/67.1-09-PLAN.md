<objective>
Apply Plan 08's D-14 wing-scale contract to the six wings that already compute their camera clearance from live hardware (`interaction.collapsedNotchSize`) via an explicit `cameraBlockWidth` term: `osdWings`, `capsLockWings`, `updateWings`, `downloadWings`, `timerWings`, `meetingWings`. Unlike Plan 08's four Spacer()-based wings, these six have a REAL hardware-verified camera floor already — `cameraBlockWidth` (and each wing's fixed `margin`) must stay completely untouched by the manual slider at every position, so this plan scales ONLY the outermost `leadingPad`/`trailingPad` constants (the true "footprint padding," per CONTEXT.md's "Claude's Discretion" simpler option), leaving `cameraBlockWidth`, `margin`, and every icon/text/bar/pill CONTENT width fixed. This is the safest possible interpretation of D-14's non-negotiable camera-safety floor: the real hardware-derived clearance is never multiplied by anything, at any slider position, ever.

Depth scaling additionally requires multiplying each wing's own inner `.frame(height: Self.wingsSize.height, ...)` content-box references by the SAME `resolvedWingDepthScale` used in the outer `wingsShape` call — without this, shrinking the depth slider below 1.0 would make the (unscaled) inner content boxes taller than the (scaled-down) outer wing frame, risking vertical overflow past the pill's rounded edge (no `.clipShape` exists in this render chain to save it).

`meetingWings` needs one extra precaution: its `iconGap` (4pt, between the elapsed-time text and the mute icon) is embedded in the LOCKED static `Self.meetingWingRightContentWidth` (=84) that `NotchWindowController.collapsedInteractiveZone()`/`meetingClickThroughZones()` also read to compute the mute icon's real click-through position — `iconGap` must stay completely unscaled so that invariant (and meetingWings' own `assert(muteIconWidth == Self.meetingMuteIconWidth, ...)`) never breaks.

Purpose: Cover the remaining 6 of the 10 named wingsShape-routed wing functions from CONTEXT.md's canonical_refs list.
Output: All 10 wingsShape-routed wings now respond to the Width/Depth sliders' D-14 scale, with camera-safety guaranteed by construction at every slider position.
</objective>
