<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 1: On-device confirmation — chevron, drag, reveal/hide, persistence</name>
  <what-built>
    A `MenuBarOverflowController` (Plan 66-02) that constructs two `NSStatusItem`s at launch — a visible chevron (leftmost among Islet's items, no Settings toggle, no permission prompt) and an invisible spacer whose `.length` toggles between 20pt (revealed) and a screen-width-clamped value (hidden) when the chevron is clicked. Both items carry a stable `autosaveName` so their position — and therefore the hidden/visible boundary — should survive a relaunch. The stale Settings toggle and superseded private-API spike files are removed (Plan 66-03).
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    1. Build and run Islet on real hardware (`xcodebuild build -scheme Islet -configuration Release -destination 'platform=macOS'`, then launch the built app).
    2. Confirm a chevron icon (`chevron.left` glyph) appears in the menu bar, positioned leftmost among Islet's own menu-bar items — MENUBAR-01.
    3. Confirm Settings has NO Menübar-Overflow card/toggle anywhere (D-02/D-04) — Settings → the Activities grid should not list it at all.
    4. Cmd-drag a real third-party app's menu-bar icon (any app you have running, e.g. a system menu extra) leftward, past the chevron — MENUBAR-02. Confirm it visually moves to the left of the chevron.
    5. Click the chevron. Confirm the glyph swaps to `chevron.right` and the icon you just dragged reappears inline in the visible strip, with other icons visibly shifting to make room — this is the MENUBAR-03 "genuine reclamation, not occlusion" check (RESEARCH.md Pitfall 3): the released space should be real, not merely an icon drawn on top of another.
    6. Click the chevron again. Confirm the glyph swaps back to `chevron.left` and the dragged icon disappears from the visible strip entirely (not just covered — check that OTHER icons to its right shift back to fill the space).
    7. Quit and relaunch Islet. Confirm the chevron reappears in the same leftmost position, and the hidden/visible grouping from step 4-6 is preserved (D-03).
    8. (If a second display is available) Connect/disconnect it once while the hidden section is in the "hidden" (expanded-spacer) state; confirm no visual corruption or icon loss occurs (Pitfall 4's screen-width-clamp recompute).
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" if all 8 steps pass as described, or describe exactly which step failed and what was observed instead (e.g. "step 5: icon reappeared but nothing shifted — looks occluded, not reclaimed").</resume-signal>
