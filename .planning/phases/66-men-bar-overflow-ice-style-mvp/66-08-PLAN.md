  <name>Task 1: Consolidated on-device UAT checkpoint</name>
  <what-built>
    The complete Menübar-Overflow mechanism: a gated, restored private-CGS enumeration/move shim
    (Plan 66-05); a rewired `MenuBarOverflowController` driving real hide/reveal via CGS repositioning
    plus a persisted, actively-re-applied third-party assignment store (Plan 66-06); and a one-time
    permission popover + Settings row pair for the Accessibility gate (Plan 66-07).
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Perform these steps in order, on real hardware, in one session — including one deliberate
    sleep/wake interruption at Step 9. Use a Release build
    (`xcodebuild build -scheme Islet -configuration Release -destination 'platform=macOS'`) or a
    normal Debug run launched via `open`/double-click — not Cmd-U for any of this.

    1. **Permission gate, denied state.** `tccutil reset Accessibility com.lippi304.islet`, then
       launch Islet. Confirm: no chevron appears anywhere in the menu bar (genuinely absent, not
       grayed out); the one-time popover appears anchored to Islet's main status icon with the
       exact "Menu Bar Overflow" / Accessibility-access copy; open Settings and confirm the
       "Menu Bar Overflow" row appears with the red "Accessibility Needed" pill.
    2. **Grant + re-launch.** Click the popover's "Open System Settings" button, grant Accessibility
       to Islet in the System Settings pane that opens, quit and relaunch Islet. Confirm: the
       chevron now appears, leftmost among Islet's own menu-bar items; the Settings row from Step 1
       is gone; the popover does NOT reappear.
    3. **Drag-to-assign (MENUBAR-02).** With the chevron in its default state, Cmd-drag a real
       third-party menu-bar icon leftward, past the chevron's position, so it visually sits to the
       chevron's left in the menu bar's own ordering.
    4. **Hide (MENUBAR-03, reclaim half).** Click the chevron. Confirm: the glyph swaps direction;
       the dragged icon disappears from the strip entirely; the icon(s) that were to its right shift
       left to fill the vacated space (reclaimed, not merely covered/occluded — compare before/after
       screenshots if the shift is subtle).
    5. **Reveal (MENUBAR-03, restore half).** Click the chevron again. Confirm: the glyph swaps back;
       the previously-hidden icon reappears inline, immediately left of the chevron.
    6. **Persistence across relaunch (D-03).** With that icon still assigned hidden, quit Islet
       entirely and relaunch it. Confirm: the icon is immediately hidden again at launch, before any
       click — this is the active re-apply pass, not a coincidence of OS ordering.
    7. **Un-hide via drag (D-03 promote-out flow).** While revealed, Cmd-drag that same icon back to
       the right of the chevron's position. Click hide, then click reveal. Confirm: the icon now
       stays visible/normal in both states — it is no longer part of the hidden section.
    8. **Repeat the permission-deny check once more.** `tccutil reset Accessibility com.lippi304.islet`
       again, relaunch. Confirm: chevron disappears again, Settings row reappears, but the one-time
       popover does NOT show a second time (the persisted show-once flag holds even across a
       permission revoke).
    9. **Sleep/wake resilience.** Re-grant Accessibility to Islet and relaunch (restoring the working
       state from Steps 2-7). With Islet running and at least one icon assigned hidden, put the Mac
       to sleep once (Apple menu > Sleep, or `pmset sleepnow`) and wake it. Without relaunching Islet,
       click the chevron to reveal, then click again to hide. Confirm both transitions still work
       exactly as in Steps 4-5 — no crash, no stale/frozen icon state, no need for a relaunch to
       recover. This confirms ROADMAP.md Success Criterion #1's sleep/wake case for the full built
       mechanism, not just the Wave-0 spike (Plan 66-05 Task 2 already checked enumeration alone;
       this step checks the complete hide/reveal/persistence path survives sleep/wake too).
  </how-to-verify>

  </resume-signal>
