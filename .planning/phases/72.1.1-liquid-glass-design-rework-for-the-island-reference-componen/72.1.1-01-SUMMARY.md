## Known Issues

**Rim/base animation-curve desync (surfaced during Task 2 on-device UAT, not part of D-04):**
- **Symptom (user, verbatim):** "Ja aussehen passt schon, problem da ist eher noch das es eben dieses helle nicht mit dem schwarzen synced ist und das schwarze eine ganz andere Animation hat." — the bright glass rim highlight and the black island base morph on visibly different animation curves/timing, desyncing mid-transition.
- **Scope assessment:** Out of scope for this plan. Plan 01's target is D-04 transition *continuity* (no flat-black frame) — confirmed fixed. The desync is a distinct defect: the black base (`matchedGeometryEffect(id: "island", in: ns)` + explicit spring) and the native `.glassEffect()` rim (its own internal SwiftUI glass-transition timing) are two independently-animated layers with different implicit curves. Root-causing/fixing an animation-curve mismatch without on-device iteration risks repeating the round-2 blind-iteration regression the resolved debug file explicitly warns against.
- **Expected resolution path:** Flagged for **Plan 72.1.1-03** (full-surface glass coverage) to re-check during its own on-device UAT — once the base surface itself becomes glass rather than a separate black fill under a glass rim, there are no longer two independently-timed layers to desync; the issue may resolve as a structural byproduct. If it persists after Plan 03, it should be raised as its own follow-up item at that point.
- **User decision:** Approved proceeding — this does not block Plan 01's checkpoint or requirements (GLASS-04/GLASS-03), which concern transition continuity, not animation-curve synchronization.
