## Idea

Inspired by the macOS app "Folder Preview" (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/folder-preview/id6698876601?mt=12).

Native macOS Quick Look (spacebar) on a folder or `.zip` today just shows the folder/zip icon large — no content. This app replaces that: spacebar on a folder or zip shows what's inside without opening it (Finder or an archive tool).

User wants the same behavior integrated into the notch/Islet's own preview flow — see file contents of a folder or archive on a quick key press, close again on release, no full window opened.

## Why

Directly useful, low novelty risk — likely a Quick Look extension (`QLPreviewProvider`) or similar system hook rather than deep new subsystem work.

## Priority

Rank 1 of the two ideas from this session — user's own assessment is this is the easier build.
